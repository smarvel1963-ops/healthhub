Health Hub privacy policy

Health Hub is an app by Marvel Corp, part of the Hub family of apps. Last updated October 22, 2026.

The short version

What you log in Health Hub stays on your phone. We have no Health Hub account system and no database of your health information. There are no ads, no ad trackers and no analytics in the app. Your health information is never sold, never used for advertising and never shared with insurers, employers or data brokers. Two optional AI features send some of it to an AI service to answer you - only when you use them - and each one is listed below.

Health Hub is a place to keep your own records and general information. It is not medical advice and does not diagnose or treat anything. Ask your doctor or pharmacist about your health and your medicines.

What stays on your phone

Everything you type: your medicines (name, strength, label instructions, times, pills left, pharmacy, prescriber) and the doses you mark taken or skipped, appointments and the questions and notes for them, vitals (blood pressure, glucose, weight, heart rate, oxygen), sleep, water, steps (you type them in - the app does not read your phone's step counter or any health service), workouts, daily check-ins (mood, energy, symptoms, notes), your emergency card (allergies, conditions, blood type, doctor, hospital, emergency contacts), goals and settings. These are saved in your browser's storage on your phone. We cannot see them.

Photos you add to the Records vault (insurance card, prescriptions, lab results and so on) are kept in a separate store on your phone. They are never sent anywhere and are never put in the Google Drive backup.

If other Hub apps are on the same phone, they can show a short read-only summary from Health Hub (and Health Hub from them); that happens on the phone and nothing is sent to us.

Deleting: ⚙ Settings → Your data → Erase everything deletes your Health Hub log on the phone, signs the phone out of Google, tells Google to drop that sign-in, and forgets saved purchase emails and the AI passphrase for every Hub app on the phone. Records-vault photos are deleted one by one in the Records vault (do that before you erase), or all at once by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app.

Friend step challenges

Step challenges with friends are shown as "coming soon" and are not switched on. Nothing is sent for them. Before they are, this page will say exactly what they send (the plan is only a nickname you pick and your daily step totals - never your medicines, vitals or anything else).

Google user data

Health Hub's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only to provide the features you turned on, in the app, on your phone. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not used to train AI models, and no person at Marvel Corp reads it. To disconnect, use ⚙ Settings in Health Hub or remove the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The backup copy can be deleted from Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps.

Children

Health Hub is for adults (18 and older). It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Selling data

We do not sell or rent personal information, and we never sell, rent or share health information for advertising or any other purpose. We hold almost none of it.

Changes

If what Health Hub sends anywhere changes, this page changes first, and the date at the top changes with it.
